How to install

  1. Click the button above to download manifest.xml
  2. Open Excel (Desktop or Office 365 Web)
  3. Go to Insert → Add-ins → Upload My Add-in
  4. Browse to the downloaded manifest.xml and click Upload
  5. Click Shift Sync on the Home tab to get started
